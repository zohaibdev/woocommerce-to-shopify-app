Response times

We answer during normal business days. Most messages get a reply within one business day; Pro and Enterprise plans are prioritised. These are targets, not guarantees.

Before you write about a failed migration

Including these makes it much faster for us to help:

  • Your Shopify store domain.
  • The migration number (shown at the top of the migration page).
  • The exact error text from the Activity log or the failed-items list.
  • Whether your WooCommerce store is publicly reachable over HTTPS.

You can also download a full report of any migration from the migration page — attach it and we can usually tell you what happened without any back and forth.
