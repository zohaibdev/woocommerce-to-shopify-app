Getting started

Connect WooCommerce in under a minute

Paste your store URL and a read-only REST API key from WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API. Shift Shop tests the connection before saving anything — if it fails, your existing setup is left untouched.

Full control

Choose exactly what migrates

Pick products, categories, customers, orders, coupons, blogs or reviews individually — the wizard shows how many records exist and how many your plan covers before you start. Set your weight unit, image size and custom tags too, so the data lands the way your store expects.

Products, variants & images

Your catalogue, not a stripped-down copy

Titles, descriptions, SKUs, prices, compare-at pricing, inventory and weight move across intact. Variable products bring their variations and options with them, and product and variant images are attached as each product is created — no separate media import step.

Customers & orders

Your sales history stays intact

Customer profiles, addresses and phone numbers carry over — phone numbers reformatted to what Shopify requires. Historical orders transfer with their line items, totals, shipping and financial status kept on their original dates, so nothing in your history goes missing.

Beyond the catalogue

Reviews, blogs and coupons aren't an afterthought

Product reviews move across as rating, author, date and content — ready to show once you add a reviews app, since Shopify's Admin API has no native reviews field. WordPress blog posts keep their original publish date and images. And supported WooCommerce coupon types become Shopify discount codes automatically.

How it works

Four steps. Then close the tab — the migration keeps running.

1

Install from the Shopify App Store

Shift Shop runs inside your Shopify admin. Nothing to host, nothing to configure.

2

Connect WooCommerce

In WooCommerce go to Settings → Advanced → REST API and create a key with Read access. Paste the store URL, consumer key and secret, then test the connection.

3

Choose what to migrate

Pick products, categories, customers, orders, coupons, blogs or reviews individually. The wizard shows exactly how many records exist and how many your plan covers before you start.

4

Start, then walk away

Everything runs on a background queue with live progress. Close the browser, log out, come back tomorrow — the run is unaffected, and we can email you when it finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Get in touch.

Does it create duplicates if I run it twice?

No. Every migrated record is mapped to the WooCommerce record it came from. A second run updates the existing Shopify record instead of creating another one — and repairs anything you deleted in Shopify by mistake.

Can I close the browser while it runs?

Yes. Migrations run on a background queue on our servers, not in your browser. Closing the tab, logging out or losing your connection has no effect on the run.

Are customer passwords migrated?

No — that is technically impossible. WooCommerce and Shopify store password hashes in incompatible formats. Your customers will need to set a password on their first Shopify login.

What happens to records that fail?

The migration keeps going. Failed records are listed with the exact reason Shopify gave, and you can retry just those records without re-running everything.

What does my WooCommerce store need?

WordPress 5.6+ with WooCommerce 5.0+, and REST API credentials with Read access. The API has to be reachable from the internet over HTTPS — stores behind a firewall, password protection or an IP allowlist cannot be read.

Are product reviews migrated?

Yes — each product's reviews (rating, author, date, verified status and content) are saved as data on the product. Shopify's Admin API has no native reviews field, so you'll need a reviews app or theme block to display them, but the data is preserved rather than lost.

What happens to my coupons and discount rules?

Percentage and fixed-amount coupons become Shopify discount codes automatically. A few WooCommerce-only restrictions — like limiting a coupon to specific customers or excluding certain products — aren't supported by Shopify's discount API. Those are named in a warning on the record so you know exactly what to recreate by hand.

Do you keep a copy of my store data?

No. We store the mapping between your WooCommerce IDs and the Shopify IDs we created, plus progress and error logs. We do not keep your product catalogue or customer records, and we never share anything with third parties. See the privacy policy.
