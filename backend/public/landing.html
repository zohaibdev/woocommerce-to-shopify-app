Common questions

Will it duplicate my products if I run it twice?

No. Every migrated record is mapped to its WooCommerce source, so a second run updates the existing product instead of creating a new one.

What happens if I close the browser?

Nothing — migrations run as background jobs on the server. Close the tab, log out, or come back tomorrow; progress is saved and keeps going.

Do customer passwords come across?

They can't — WooCommerce and Shopify hash passwords differently. Customers are created with their profile and addresses, and can reset their password or be sent an invite.

What if some items fail?

The migration keeps going. Failed items are listed with the exact reason and can be retried on their own, without touching what already succeeded.
