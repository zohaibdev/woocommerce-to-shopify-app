1. The service

Store Shift is a Shopify application that copies store data — products, categories, customers and orders — from a WooCommerce store you control into a Shopify store you control. It is a one-way copying tool. It does not keep the two stores in sync, does not migrate themes, apps, page content, discount codes, reviews or passwords, and does not modify or delete anything in your WooCommerce store.

3. Your responsibilities
